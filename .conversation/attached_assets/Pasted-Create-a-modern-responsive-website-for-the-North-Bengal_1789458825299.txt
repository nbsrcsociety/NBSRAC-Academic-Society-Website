Create a modern, responsive website for the North Bengal Society for 
Research and Academic Collaboration (NBSRAC), a society promoting research 
excellence, academic collaboration, and knowledge exchange at regional, 
national, and international levels, with a focus on strengthening the 
research and academic ecosystem in North Bengal.

Design: Clean, professional, academic/institutional style — trustworthy 
and credible, not flashy. 
Color scheme: Navy blue and white (or suggest an alternative that fits an 
academic society). 
Fully responsive for mobile and desktop.

Include the following pages/sections:

1. Home
   - Hero section with society name, tagline (e.g. "Advancing Research, 
     Building Collaboration"), and a "Become a Member" / "Learn More" CTA
   - Short "About" summary
   - Highlights strip: e.g. number of member institutions, focus areas, 
     upcoming events

2. About
   - Aim of the Society (promoting research excellence, academic 
     collaboration, and knowledge exchange, with special focus on North 
     Bengal)
   - Background/history (formed as a core committee, currently undergoing 
     formal registration)
   - Vision and values

3. Objectives
   - Present the 10 objective categories as cards or an accordion:
     Research & Innovation, Academic Collaboration, Academic Events, 
     Publication & Dissemination, Support to Researchers & Students, 
     Regional Development, Networking & Knowledge Exchange, Institutional 
     Support Services, Capacity Building & Training, Collaboration with 
     Government & Agencies
   - Short description under each (1-2 lines)

4. Leadership & Committee
   - President and Secretary featured at top (name, photo placeholder, title)
   - Core Committee grid: name, designation, institution, contact (photo 
     placeholder, name, title, institution — link email as a mailto icon, 
     not shown in plaintext)
   - Separate "Recently Joined Members" section if there's a natural cadence 
     of onboarding new members

5. Activities & Events
   - Placeholder cards for conferences, seminars, workshops, FDPs the 
     Society organizes/supports
   - "Past Events" vs "Upcoming Events" toggle or tabs
   - CTA to propose/host an event with the Society's support

6. Publications
   - Placeholder listing for journals, conference proceedings, edited 
     volumes, and technical reports the Society publishes or supports
   - Filter by year/type if content grows

7. Membership
   - Who can join (institutions, researchers, students)
   - Benefits of membership
   - Membership application form: name, designation, institution, email, 
     phone, area of interest
   - Note: "Society registration in progress" disclaimer if relevant

8. Collaborations & Partners
   - Logo grid of partner universities/colleges/institutions
   - MoU highlights (placeholder)

9. FAQ
   - Accordion-style: how to join, how to propose an event, how to 
     collaborate, membership fees, etc.

10. Contact
    - Contact form + general email + social/LinkedIn links
    - Footer with quick links (About, Objectives, Committee, Membership) 
      and copyright

Technical requirements:
- Use React with Tailwind CSS for styling
- Smooth scroll navigation with a sticky header
- Mobile hamburger menu
- Use placeholder text/images/logos where real content isn't provided 
  (e.g. member photos, partner logos, past event images)
- Keep all committee contact details as icons/mailto links rather than 
  printed emails and phone numbers, for privacy
- Before writing code, restate the plan in a few bullets so I can confirm it
